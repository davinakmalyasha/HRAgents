# Backend Engineer Evaluation Rubric

## Technical depth

Evidence of sustained building: years of hands-on work, breadth of technologies actually
used (from tech stacks and project descriptions, not skill lists alone), shipped
projects, and publications where relevant. Depth is measured by duration and by the
complexity of what was built — not by the number of keywords.

## Stack alignment

How closely the candidate's demonstrated stack matches the job's must-have skills.
Aliases count (Postgres = PostgreSQL); unrelated exposure does not. A candidate who has
used a framework in production outranks one who lists it with no context.

## Systems literacy

Evidence of thinking beyond single files: architectures (queues, caching, event-driven
design), operational concerns (observability, latency, scaling, deployment), databases,
and infrastructure. Holdings: production responsibilities, design decisions described
in first person, cross-system work.

## Verifiable certifications

Weighted toward credentials that can be checked (issuer registries, verifiable IDs).
Self-claimed certificates without verification count for less. No certificate replaces
demonstrated work.
