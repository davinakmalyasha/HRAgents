# Candidate Feedback Report Template

## Structure

1. **Opening (one sentence).** Thank them, state which role was reviewed, state that
   the review is complete and evidence-based.
2. **How you were evaluated (two sentences).** Four dimensions: technical depth, stack
   alignment, systems literacy, verifiable certifications. Evidence from submitted
   documents and linked artifacts. Human-reviewed.
3. **Strengths (2–3 bullets).** Each must cite specific evidence: technologies used in
   real roles, projects, publications, verified credentials.
4. **Development areas (1–3 bullets).** Frame as missing evidence, name what would have
   been looked for, avoid judgment of ability.
5. **Process transparency (one sentence).** State the decision is recorded and
   explainable on request.
6. **Right to correction (one sentence).** If any evidence was extracted incorrectly,
   report it by replying; the material is re-reviewed by a human.

## Example phrasing (English)

> Thank you for applying for the Backend Engineer position. We reviewed your
> application based on four evidence areas: technical depth, stack alignment, systems
> literacy, and verifiable certifications. The review is complete and was signed off
> by an engineer.
>
> **What stood out**
> - Six years of hands-on backend work, including payment services built with Python
>   and PostgreSQL.
> - Operational experience: queue-based ingestion and latency improvements.
>
> **Where the evidence was thinner**
> - Docker and container orchestration were not demonstrated in the material reviewed.
> - No verifiable cloud certification was found (you may have one we could not check —
>   feel free to share the credential ID).
>
> Your assessment is recorded and can be explained at any time. If any fact extracted
> from your documents is wrong, reply to this message and a person will re-review it.

## Example phrasing (Bahasa Indonesia)

> Terima kasih telah melamar untuk posisi Backend Engineer. Kami meninjau lamaran Anda
> berdasarkan empat area: kedalaman teknis, kesesuaian stack, pemahaman sistem, dan
> sertifikasi yang dapat diverifikasi. Peninjauan selesai dan telah ditandatangani
> oleh seorang engineer.
>
> **Yang menonjol**
> - Enam tahun pengalaman backend, termasuk layanan pembayaran dengan Python dan
>   PostgreSQL.
> - Pengalaman operasional: sistem antrean dan perbaikan latensi.
>
> **Yang buktinya belum terlihat**
> - Docker dan orkestrasi container belum terlihat di materi yang ditinjau.
> - Tidak ditemukan sertifikasi cloud yang dapat diverifikasi (jika Anda punya,
>   sertakan ID-nya).
>
> Hasil penilaian tersimpan dan dapat dijelaskan kapan saja. Jika ada data yang salah
> ekstraksi, balas pesan ini dan akan ditinjau ulang oleh manusia.
