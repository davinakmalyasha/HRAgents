# Company Profile (Template)

> Operators: replace this template with your company's actual information. The agents
> treat this document as the source of truth for company facts.

## About

Company name, what we do, size, locations, working model (onsite/hybrid/remote).

## Hiring process

Stages, expected timeline per stage, who interviews, how decisions are communicated.

## Culture and values

What we value in collaboration, communication expectations, working hours and
flexibility.

## Benefits (if shared with candidates)

Leave, insurance (BPJS plus any private coverage), allowances, learning budget,
equipment. Keep this factual and current — candidates rely on it.

## FAQ

Common candidate questions with official answers. Add entries as they repeat.

## Contacts

Who candidates can reach for questions, and response expectations.
