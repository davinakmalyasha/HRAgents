# Minimum Wage Structure

Source: the yearly gubernatorial decree for the province/regency (UMP/UMK)
under the prevailing wage regulation. The figure changes every year and
differs by region — there is no national number.

## The rule

Paying below the applicable regional minimum is a labour-law breach. The
product treats it as blocking: payroll compares the paid wage against the
operator's verified `minimum_wage` table and refuses sign-off on a shortfall
rather than paying it.

## Where the numbers live here

The minimum itself is operator data, entered and verified yearly from the
governor's decree. This note never states a wage figure.

This is a summary for HR orientation, not legal advice — confirm the current
decree for your region.
