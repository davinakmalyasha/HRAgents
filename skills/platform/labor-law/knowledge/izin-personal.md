# Izin — Paid Personal Leave

Source: UU No. 13 Tahun 2003 tentang Ketenagakerjaan, Pasal 93 (as amended;
confirm current wording against the official JDIH text for your case).

## The rule

Certain personal events carry statutory paid leave, including: the
employee's own marriage (3 days); marrying off a child, circumcising a child,
or baptizing a child (2 days each); a spouse giving birth or miscarrying
(2 days); the death of a spouse, parent, parent-in-law, child, or
child-in-law (2 days); and the death of another household member (1 day).

## Where the numbers live here

The event list and day counts above are the statutory structure. How each
event maps to a leave request (which leave type, what evidence) is operator
data: see the leave policies in the Leave workspace.
