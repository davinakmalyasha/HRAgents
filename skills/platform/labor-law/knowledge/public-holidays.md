# Public Holidays and Cuti Bersama

Source: the yearly joint ministerial decree (SKB Menteri Agama, Menteri
Ketenagakerjaan, dan Menteri PANRB) on national holidays and collective
leave. The list changes every year — there is no fixed statutory calendar.

## The rule

National holidays are non-working days. Collective leave (cuti bersama,
typically around religious holidays) is also announced yearly and usually
deducted from annual-leave entitlement under the prevailing rules.

## Where the numbers live here

The product ships no statutory dates. The operator enters each year's
holidays into the holiday calendar (Leave workspace), and working-day math —
leave balances, attendance absence, payroll proration — reads that calendar.
When a new SKB is published, updating the calendar is an HR task with a
named human behind it.

This is a summary for HR orientation, not legal advice — confirm the year's
SKB for your case.
