# Working Time and Overtime

Source: UU No. 13 Tahun 2003 with implementing rules in PP No. 35 Tahun 2021.
Confirm current wording against the official JDIH text for your case.

## The rule

Standard working time is 40 hours per week: either 7 hours a day over 6 days
or 8 hours a day over 5 days. Overtime requires an employer order and the
employee's consent, is capped per day and per week, and is paid in tiers:
the first overtime hour of a day at one premium tier, subsequent hours at a
higher tier, with distinct treatment for rest days and public holidays.

## Where the numbers live here

Tier multipliers, the monthly-hours divisor, and the caps are operator data:
the verified `overtime_premium` rate table. This note states the structure
only — payroll prices overtime from that table and blocks when it is
missing or unverified. Attendance import computes hours past 8 per day from
fingerprint exports.

This is a summary of the cited sources for HR orientation, not legal advice
for a specific case — confirm with the operator or counsel.
