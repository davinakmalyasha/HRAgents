# Cuti Sakit — Sick Leave

Source: UU No. 13 Tahun 2003 tentang Ketenagakerjaan, Pasal 93 (as amended;
confirm current wording against the official JDIH text for your case).

## The rule

An employee who is ill and cannot work is paid during sick leave. Longer
absences require a doctor's certificate (surat keterangan dokter); the exact
evidence threshold is the company's own policy. Prolonged illness has
statutory protections that go beyond ordinary sick days — treat those cases
as legal matters, not routine approvals.

## Where the numbers live here

Whether a supporting document is required, and any per-request caps, are
operator data: see the leave policy for the sick-leave type. The document
vault holds the certificate; the approval queue holds the decision.

This is a summary of the cited source for HR orientation, not legal advice
for a specific case — confirm with the operator or counsel.
