# Probation and PKWT

Source: UU No. 13 Tahun 2003, Pasal 58–59, with implementing rules in
PP No. 35 Tahun 2021. Confirm current wording against the official JDIH text
for your case.

## The rule

A probation period (masa percobaan) may only be agreed for indefinite-term
contracts (PKWTT): at most 3 months, once, and it must be stated in writing —
without a written clause there is no probation. During probation the wage
must not fall below the applicable minimum. Fixed-term contracts (PKWT) are
for work that is by nature temporary; their permitted scope and maximum
duration are set by the implementing regulation, and a PKWT that violates
those limits converts by law.
