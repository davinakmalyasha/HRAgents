# THR — Religious-Holiday Bonus

Source: Peraturan Menteri Ketenagakerjaan No. 6 Tahun 2016. Confirm current
wording against the official JDIH text for your case.

## The rule

THR is mandatory for employees with at least one month of continuous service.
It must be paid no later than 7 days before the religious holiday. An
employee with 12 months of service receives one month's wage; shorter
service is prorated by months of service over 12. Late or missing THR carries
administrative sanctions for the employer.

## Where the numbers live here

Wage figures and the service-length ladder are operator data: the verified
`thr_formula` rate table and each payroll input's service months. A THR run
computes the entitlement from those and blocks when the table is missing or
unverified. This note states timing and structure only.

This is a summary of the cited source for HR orientation, not legal advice
for a specific case — confirm with the operator or counsel.
