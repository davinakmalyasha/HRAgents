# Resignation, Termination, and Final Pay

Source: UU No. 13 Tahun 2003 with termination rules in PP No. 35 Tahun 2021.
Confirm current wording against the official JDIH text for your case —
termination is the highest-stakes area in this pack.

## The structure

Resignation requires written notice 30 days ahead, during which duties
continue. Termination (PHK) is lawful only on statutory grounds with the
prescribed procedure; unlawful termination is a dispute, not paperwork.
Severance (pesangon), service-period rewards, and compensation rights follow
a statutory ladder keyed to tenure and termination ground. Final pay,
outstanding leave, and asset return are coordinated — never executed — by
the product.

## Where the numbers live here

Tenure ladders and payout figures are operator data: the verified
`thr_formula` table for termination-scale runs and the offboarding checklist
for clearance. The offboarding workspace gates finalization on required steps
and asset return, and a named human signs off. This note never states a
severance figure.

This is a summary of the cited sources for HR orientation, not legal advice
for a specific case — termination cases go to counsel, always.
