# Cuti Tahunan — Annual Leave

Source: UU No. 13 Tahun 2003 tentang Ketenagakerjaan, Pasal 79 (as amended;
confirm current wording against the official JDIH text for your case).

## The rule

An employee with 12 months of continuous service is entitled to 12 working
days of annual leave. Leave timing is agreed between the employer and the
employee; unused leave handling (carryover, expiry, payout) follows the
company's own leave policy, not the statute alone.

## Where the numbers live here

The yearly entitlement, accrual method, carryover cap, and minimum service
months are operator data: see the leave policy for the leave type in the
Leave workspace. Payroll and balances compute from that policy, never from
this note.

This is a summary of the cited source for HR orientation, not legal advice
for a specific case — confirm with the operator or counsel.
