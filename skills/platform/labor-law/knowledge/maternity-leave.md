# Maternity and Paternity Leave

Source: UU No. 13 Tahun 2003, Pasal 82–83, as amended by the Cipta Kerja law
(UU No. 6 Tahun 2023). Confirm current wording against the official JDIH text
for your case — this area has been amended more than once.

## The rule

A pregnant employee is entitled to rest before and after childbirth, in the
order of one and a half months each, extendable with a doctor's certificate.
An employee who miscarries is entitled to one and a half months of rest. A
father is entitled to 2 days of paid leave when his spouse gives birth, with
longer accompaniment possible by agreement.

## Where the numbers live here

Month and day counts above are the statutory structure; pay treatment during
the leave follows the company's payroll setup. Individual cases (extensions,
medical certificates, return-to-work scheduling) go through a human, not a
template answer.

This is a summary of the cited source for HR orientation, not legal advice
for a specific case — confirm with the operator or counsel.
