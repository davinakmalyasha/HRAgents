# UU PDP No. 27/2022 — Key Obligations for Hiring

Source: Undang-Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi, effective
17 October 2022 (LN.2022/No.196, TLN No.6820). Verified from the official JDIH/BPK
database.

## Scope

Candidate CVs, contact details, photos, assessment results, and communication records
are personal data. The employer (or the HR platform acting for them) processes this
data as a controller/processor under UU PDP.

## Data categories

- **Specific**: health, biometric, genetic, criminal records, children's data, personal
  financial data.
- **General**: name, gender, nationality, religion, marital status, and combinations
  that identify a person.

Hiring should collect only general data strictly necessary for the decision, and avoid
specific categories entirely.

## Data-subject rights

Candidates have the right to: information about processing, access their data,
correction, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability. These rights require
operational support: export, correction workflows, and deletion jobs.

## Processing principles

Lawful basis and consent, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage
limitation (retention windows), security, and accountability.

## Consequences of non-compliance

Administrative sanctions (warnings, fines, processing suspension, deletion orders) and
criminal provisions for unlawful processing or disclosure.

## Practical rules for this system

1. Consent recorded before extraction; revocation halts processing immediately.
2. Retention windows configured per data type; deletion runs on schedule.
3. Assessment outputs (scores, feedback, flags) are personal data with the same rights.
4. Breach workflow: if data is exposed, escalate to the operator immediately — do not
   attempt remediation yourself.
5. Cross-border transfer (e.g., calling an overseas AI provider) must be authorized by
   the operator's configuration; prefer local models when configured for maximum
   privacy.
